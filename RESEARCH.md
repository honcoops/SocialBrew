# Social Brew Website — Research & Feature List

## About Social Brew

- **Name:** Social Brew
- **Type:** Craft beer tasting bar (curates & serves, does not brew on-site)
- **Address:** 140 N Main St, Crossville, TN 38555
- **Phone:** (931) 456-2739
- **Hours:** Wed 3–9pm, Thu 3–10pm, Fri–Sat 3–11pm (Closed Sun–Tue)
- **Taps:** 10 rotating taps — Tennessee breweries (Calfkiller, Yee-Haw), ciders (Blake's Hard Cider), specialty/seasonal rotations focused on unique beers, ciders, sours, and porters
- **Cans/Bottles:** 60+ in cooler for on-site or retail purchase
- **Food:** No kitchen — BYOF, delivery welcome, occasional food trucks
- **Events:** Live music (Fridays), trivia, karaoke
- **Vibe:** Relaxed downtown gathering spot. Board games, TVs, indoor/outdoor seating. Pet-friendly & family-friendly.
- **Current Online Presence:** Facebook (5,898 likes), Yelp, Untappd, TripAdvisor, Explore Crossville listing — **no dedicated website**
- **Facebook:** https://www.facebook.com/SocialBrewCrossville/
- **Untappd:** https://untappd.com/v/social-brew/3607999
- **Has a pour wall** (self-serve tap wall)

### Brand Identity (from Facebook/online)
- Described as "relaxed and welcoming" with "laid-back charm" and "friendly vibe"
- Positioned as a community gathering place — "where old friends and new gather"
- Indoor and outdoor seating
- Downtown Crossville location — walkable, accessible

## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Local Competitors

#### 1. Swing On Brewing Co (Crossville, TN)
- **Type:** Microbrewery with taproom
- **Location:** Potato Farm Rd, Crossville (countryside, not downtown)
- **Taps:** 11 beers on tap (brews their own)
- **Differentiators:** Grows own hops, Caddyshack theme, golf simulator
- **Website:** Has one — gives them online advantage
- **Social Brew advantage:** Downtown location, curated variety vs single-brewery

#### 2. Creekside Brewing Co (Crossville, TN)
- **Type:** Nano brewery with taproom
- **Location:** 11750 Hwy 127 S, Crossville
- **Note:** Seasonal operation (closes winters, reopens spring ~March)
- **Social Brew advantage:** Year-round operation, downtown location, wider selection

### Regional Competitors

#### 3. Cofer's Garage (Rockwood, TN — ~30 min away)
- **Type:** Beer garden & events venue (curates, doesn't brew — same model as Social Brew)
- **Address:** 141 S Front St, Rockwood, TN 37854
- **Hours:** Thu–Sat only
- **Website:** cofersgarage.com — well-designed with dark charcoal tones, Fjalla One headings
- **Features:**
  - Events calendar
  - "Cup Club" loyalty program
  - Mobile beer trailer (Cofer's 2.0)
  - Cigars & Guitars event series
  - FAQ page
  - Reviews page
  - Crew/team page
- **Design:** Dark charcoal backgrounds, warm taupe accents, sticky header, mobile-responsive
- **Tagline:** "Rockwood's Favorite Beer Garden & Events Venue"
- **Vibe:** Family-friendly, inclusive ("EVERYONE is welcome!"), has 3 spaces (Service Dept, Main Garage, Beer Garden)
- **What they do well:** Strong website, clear branding, events-driven, loyalty program
- **Social Brew opportunity:** More days open, downtown walkability, larger tap selection, pour wall

#### 4. Calfkiller Brewing Company (Sparta, TN — ~35 min away)
- **Type:** Craft brewery (brews on-site)
- **Address:** 1839 Blue Springs Rd, Sparta, TN
- **Website:** calfkillerbeer.com (basic/dated design)
- **Beers:** 20 beer rotation, 5 staples always on tap + seasonals
- **Notable brews:** Grassroots Pale Ale, Deadhorse Porter, Smokey Treat Smoked Barley Ale
- **Features:**
  - Beer list page
  - Online merch store (Storenvy)
  - Brewery tours (Saturdays by appointment)
  - Food truck on-site
  - Gift shop with growlers
- **Motto:** "Unparalleled Drinkability, Paradoxically Balanced by Uncompromised Complexity"
- **What they do well:** Known brand (Social Brew even carries their beer), established brewery cred
- **Social Brew opportunity:** Better website design, more modern UX, events focus, curated multi-brewery selection

### Additional Area Competitors
- **Lilly Pad Hopyard Brewery** — Small batch, near Obed Wild & Scenic River National Park

## Tech Stack Decision

**Framework:** Astro
- Ships zero JS by default — fast, static pages
- JSON data files for tap list management (edit + redeploy)
- Interactive "islands" only where needed (age gate, tap list filters)
- Deploys to any static host (Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages)
- Component-based, clean, maintainable

**Tap List Management:** JSON data files
- `/src/data/taps.json` — current on-tap beers
- `/src/data/cans.json` — packaged beer inventory
- `/src/data/events.json` — upcoming events
- Easy to update without touching code

**Hosting:** Static (Netlify, Vercel, or Cloudflare Pages — all free tier)

## Design Direction

Based on Social Brew's brand personality and competitive positioning:
- **Warm, inviting palette** — earthy tones reflecting the relaxed downtown vibe
- **Clean, modern layout** — beat Calfkiller's dated site, match/exceed Cofer's quality
- **Photography-forward** — placeholder hero sections ready for real venue photos
- **Community-centered messaging** — "where old friends and new gather"
- **Highlight the pour wall** — unique differentiator, should be prominent
